MY SHOP Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

MY SHOP respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the MY SHOP application, website, live streaming, social, shopping, messaging, rewards, and related services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, MY SHOP may collect information such as:

  • Name, user ID, profile information and profile photo
  • Email address or other account information
  • Account activity and preferences
  • Posts, comments, messages and other user-generated content
  • Purchase, order, transaction and reward-related information
  • Coin and Gold Coin transaction records
  • Live streaming and interaction information
  • Device, application, diagnostic and security information
  • Information you voluntarily provide to customer support

2. Camera and Microphone

MY SHOP may request access to your camera and microphone when required for features such as live streaming, audio participation, profile photos, image/video uploads, or other communication features. These permissions are used only when required to provide the corresponding functionality.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts
  • Provide shopping and social features
  • Provide live streaming and communication services
  • Process transactions and maintain transaction records
  • Provide notifications and service updates
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity
  • Handle reports, blocking and moderation
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve reliability, security and user experience
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

4. Coin and Gold Coin

MY SHOP may provide virtual balances known as Coin and Gold Coin. Transaction records relating to these balances may be maintained for accounting, security, fraud prevention and customer support purposes. Purchased virtual balances and earned balances may be handled separately according to MY SHOP's applicable rules and policies.

5. Live Streaming and Social Features

When you participate in public or interactive features, information such as your profile name, profile photo, user ID, live activity, comments, reactions, gifts or other interactions may be visible to other users as part of the service.

6. Messages and User Content

Users may be able to create posts, upload content, communicate with other users, or participate in live interactions. Users are responsible for the content they choose to submit or make public.

7. Notifications

With your permission, MY SHOP may send notifications relating to messages, live activity, account activity, orders, security, announcements or other service-related events. Notification permission can be controlled through your device settings where supported.

8. Service Providers

MY SHOP may use third-party service providers for functions such as hosting, authentication, live communication, notifications, storage, analytics, security, payment processing, or other infrastructure. Information may be processed by these providers only as necessary to provide their respective services and subject to applicable agreements and requirements.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss or disclosure. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain required transaction or security records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and satisfy applicable legal requirements.

11. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their MY SHOP account and associated personal information through the account deletion option provided within MY SHOP or by contacting MY SHOP support.

Some information may be retained when necessary for legitimate security, fraud prevention, transaction, dispute-resolution or legal obligations.

12. Children's Privacy

MY SHOP is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Where age-related requirements apply, MY SHOP may restrict access or take other appropriate measures.

13. Reports, Blocking and Safety

MY SHOP may provide reporting, blocking and moderation tools to help protect users and maintain the safety of the platform. Information related to reports may be reviewed and retained when reasonably necessary for investigation, enforcement, security or legal purposes.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, practices or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date where appropriate.
